# Changelog

## [0.1.0] — unreleased

First public release.

- Highlighting for the three layers of the format — what the player reads, what the engine does,
  and what you have not written yet (`___`).
- Outline, folding, hover, go to definition, find references and rename for labels and variables.
- Flow checks as you type, the same rules as the `kio-check` command line, plus undeclared names,
  markup and unknown directives.
- The scene map: the whole graph laid out, coloured by background, clickable, exportable as SVG,
  with a stage view of who is on stage where.
- Sidebar views: story, variables, cast and assets, and what is still to write.
